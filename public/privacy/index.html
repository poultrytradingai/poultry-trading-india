Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information PoultryTrading (poultrytrading.com, "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We operate from Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.

1. What we collect

  • Directory listings: business name, category, sub-category, country, state, city, address, contact person, phone or WhatsApp number, email, website, products and services, certifications, description.
  • Trade enquiries: buy or sell role, product, quantity, countries, incoterms, timeline, details, your name, company, email and phone.
  • Contact form: name, email and message.
  • Member sign-in (Google): your Google account ID, name and email address. We do not receive your Google password. You may also add a WhatsApp number to your profile.
  • Activity: which contact details you unlock and when, so we can apply daily limits and prevent misuse.
  • Technical data: your IP address, browser and device information, used for security, spam protection and basic analytics.

2. How we use it

  • To publish your directory listing and trade enquiries as described below.
  • To let signed-in members view contact details, and to enforce daily limits.
  • To verify members, prevent spam, scraping and abuse, and keep the service secure.
  • To respond to your messages and send service alerts.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.

3. What is public and what is shown to members

  • Public: for a directory listing, only the business name, country, category and sub-category. For an enquiry, only the type, product, quantity, route or country, and date, shown anonymously.
  • Members only: phone, email, website and address are shown only to signed-in members, up to a daily limit. By submitting a listing or enquiry you agree that these details can be shown to such members.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information. We use service providers to run the site:

  • Cloudflare (hosting, database and spam protection through Turnstile).
  • Google (Sheets and Apps Script to store submissions, Google Sign-In, and Tag Manager or Analytics).

These providers process data on our behalf and may do so in countries other than your own. We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

  • Session cookie: keeps you signed in as a member. It is essential for sign-in.
  • Analytics: we use Google Tag Manager and related tools to measure traffic. These may set cookies.
  • Spam protection: Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a form is submitted by a person.

6. How long we keep data

We keep listings, enquiries and member records while they are active or needed for the purposes above, then delete or anonymise them. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, to withdraw consent for showing your contact details, or to close your member account. Contact us through the contact page and tell us which listing, enquiry or account you mean. We may need to confirm your identity first. Depending on where you live, laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the EU and UK GDPR may give you additional rights.

8. Security

We use access controls, secret keys, rate limits and daily unlock limits to protect data. No online service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The service is for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: use the contact page.
