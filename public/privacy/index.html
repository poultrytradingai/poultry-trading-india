Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect through poultrytrading.com, why we collect it, who can see it, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Nawaz, a sole proprietor trading as PoultryTrading (poultrytrading.com), Mumbai, Maharashtra, India ("we", "us"), runs poultrytrading.com. We decide why and how personal data is used on this site, so we are the "data fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the "controller" where the GDPR applies.

What we collectWhy we use it

3. What is public and what members see

  • Public: for a directory listing, only the business name, country, category and sub-category. For an enquiry, only the type, product, quantity, route or country, and date, shown anonymously.
  • Signed-in members only: phone, email, website and address, up to a daily limit. Each unlock is recorded.
  • Never public: your address, phone, email and Google account details are not shown to visitors who are not signed in.

4. Consent and how to withdraw it

When you submit a form or sign in, you agree to the use of your data as described here. You can withdraw consent at any time through the grievance page. After withdrawal we will stop the processing and delete or anonymise the data, unless the law requires us to keep it. Withdrawal does not affect what we did before you withdrew.

5. Who we share data with

  • Other members: your contact details, as described above.
  • Service providers (processors): Cloudflare (hosting, database, spam protection) and Google (Sheets and Apps Script, Google Sign-In, Tag Manager and Analytics). They process data only on our instructions.
  • Authorities: where required by law or a valid court or government order.
  • Business transfer: if the business is sold or merged, with the same protections.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Transfers outside India

Our providers may store or process data on servers outside India. We rely on their security commitments and on lawful transfer mechanisms. We will not transfer data to any country the Government of India has restricted.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

  • Session cookie keeps you signed in. It is essential.
  • Analytics (Google Tag Manager and related tools) measure traffic and may set cookies.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a form is submitted by a person.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser. Blocking the session cookie stops sign-in from working.

8. How long we keep data

  • Listings and enquiries: until you ask us to remove them, we remove them as spam or abuse, or they are no longer needed.
  • Member accounts and unlock records: while your account is active and up to 24 months after your last activity, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Contact and grievance messages: up to 24 months after the matter is closed.
  • Invoice, licence and tax records: as long as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can:

  • get a summary of the personal data we hold and how we use it;
  • correct or update your data, or ask us to delete it;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • have your grievance heard and answered;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to;
  • if the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to you: also ask us to restrict or port your data, object to processing, and complain to your local data protection authority.

To use these rights, write to the Grievance Officer (section 14). We aim to reply within 15 days and always within the time the law allows. After you have raised a matter with us, you can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

10. If someone listed your details without your permission

Tell us through the grievance page with the listing or enquiry link. We will check and remove it promptly, and where it was submitted without your consent we will delete the contact details.

11. Security and breaches

We use HTTPS, access controls, secret keys, spam protection, rate limits and daily unlock limits. No online service is completely secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

12. Children

The site is for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has given us data, tell us and we will delete it.

13. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above. For material changes we will tell users on the site. We also remind users of our policies and terms at least once a year.
