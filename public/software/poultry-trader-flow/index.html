Frequently asked questions

Quick answers

Is there a free trial?

Yes, a 15-day free trial. Contact us to start, or ask for a demo.

Can I send bills on WhatsApp?

Yes, at no extra charge. Pick parties by supervisor, area, group or list, choose the period, and send statements to up to 10 parties at once.

Do I need internet to run the software?

The desktop software runs on your Windows computer without internet. The mobile app connects through a server, which has a monthly server charge.

Is there a monthly fee?

The desktop plan is a one-time payment. The mobile app has an additional server charge: ₹500 per month on Growth and ₹800 per month on Pro.

Is my data safe?

Desktop data is stored on your own computer, not on our servers. You can back it up, including to a pen drive.

What support do I get?

3 months of technical support, plus training and user guide videos on YouTube.

Can I use it if I am not good with computers?

Yes. It is designed to be simple, and training videos are available.

Is it only for Maharashtra?

It is made for Maharashtra's poultry traders and is available to traders across India. Outside India, contact us.
