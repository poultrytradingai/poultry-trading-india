Grievance Redressal

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Use this page to report content, request removal, exercise your privacy rights, or raise a complaint.

Grievance Officer

Grievance Officer, PoultryTrading (poultrytrading.com), Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. You can also write through the contact page and put "Grievance" at the start of your message.

What you can use this page for

  • Report a listing, enquiry or any content that breaks our Terms of Use or the law.
  • Ask us to remove your business or contact details.
  • Access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent (see our Privacy Policy).
  • Complain about the service, an account or a software purchase.

How to complain

Write to the Grievance Officer. Include your name, how we can reach you, the link to the page you are reporting, and what you want us to do. We may ask you to confirm your identity.

What we do

  • We acknowledge your complaint within 24 hours.
  • We aim to resolve it within 15 days and tell you the reasons for what we did.
  • For urgent content issues, such as private images shared without consent, we act within 24 hours of a complaint.
  • We act on valid court and government orders.

If you are not satisfied

You may appeal to the Grievance Appellate Committee within 30 days of our decision, where that route applies to you. For personal data matters, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India after you have first raised the matter with us.
